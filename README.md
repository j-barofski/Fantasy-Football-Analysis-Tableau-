# Fantasy-Football-Analysis-Tableau-
Analysis on the first six weeks of my fantasy football league using Tableau.

# Standings
- Shows the rankings of all the teams by their record
- Tiebreaker is the total amount of points for 
- All-play record is what a team’s record would be if they played every other team each week
- As expected, teams with better all-play records have the most wins
- MB’s team is the lone outlier here. They have a winning all-play record in losses yet a losing all-play record in wins. Also, both of their wins would be losses if they played against any other team.
